# Play Heuristics

## Combat

- Re-read `sts2 state` after every card play.
- Use lethal if available.
- Preserve HP when the block line is efficient.
- Prioritize scaling buffs and premium debuffs early in multi-turn fights.
- Prefer attacking the enemy that can be killed, heavily weakened, or is presenting the largest threat.
- Use potions when they materially change the fight outcome, avoid significant HP loss, or secure lethal.
- Do not end turn while a clearly useful play remains.

## Map

- If there is only one legal node, take it.
- Prefer safer paths by default.
- Avoid elites unless the deck and HP justify them.
- Prefer rest sites when HP is becoming a constraint.
- Prefer shops when gold is high enough to buy something meaningful.

## Rewards

- Claim gold, potion, and relic rewards before leaving the reward screen.
- Take card rewards only when they improve consistency, damage quality, scaling, or defense.
- Skip filler cards that dilute the deck.

## Shop

- Buy premium relics first.
- Card removal is often better than a mediocre card.
- Avoid low-impact purchases that consume all remaining gold.

## Events

- Prefer low-risk value.
- Avoid curses and large HP payments unless the upside is clearly worth it.

## Rest Sites

- Rest when survival is in doubt.
- Otherwise prefer upgrades or higher-value options.
